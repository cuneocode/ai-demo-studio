# Card testing

Synthetic playbook for the studio demo. Not a bank's procedure.

## Signals
- A burst of very small card-not-present authorizations within minutes, often at a donation or low-value merchant.
- A larger purchase follows soon after, frequently electronics or gift cards.
- Other cards show the same small-charge pattern at the same merchant.

## Response
- Decline or hold the large purchase pending customer contact.
- Block and reissue the card if the customer does not recognise the small charges.
- Report the testing merchant to the acquirer relations team.

## Customer contact
- Ask whether the customer recognises the small charges; most customers will not.
